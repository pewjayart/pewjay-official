THE ANGRY BIRDS MOVIE

2016

THE FIRST FLIGHT

The Angry Birds Movie introduced audiences to a colorful world filled with birds, pigs, humor, friendship, conflict, and adventure.

At the center of the story is Red, a bird whose personality and experiences make him different from the rest of his island.

Alongside Chuck and Bomb, Red becomes part of an adventure that changes the relationship between the birds and the mysterious pigs who arrive on their island.

The first movie established the cinematic world of Angry Birds and brought characters originally known from the games into a new animated feature-film setting.

THE FLOCK RETURNS

The Angry Birds Movie 3 represents the next chapter of the cinematic Angry Birds story. After the first two movies introduced audiences to the characters, their friendships, rivalries, and adventures, the third installment brings the franchise into a new era.

For fans who followed the series from its original movie to its sequel, another theatrical chapter represents an opportunity to see familiar characters return while discovering what new situations await them.

The movie also continues the connection between the Angry Birds universe and the larger world of animated entertainment. Its return creates another opportunity for audiences and longtime fans to celebrate the franchise on the big screen.
